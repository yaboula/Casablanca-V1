# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casablanca-V1 serves airport car-rental customers and the staff who operate pickups at Casablanca Mohammed V Airport. The operator surface is used by staff under time pressure, often near arrivals, where they need to verify a reservation, inspect identity and documents, confirm the assigned vehicle, and move the handoff forward without exposing unnecessary customer or payment data.

## Product Purpose

The product coordinates premium vehicle reservations from booking through document review, payment capture, smart-ticket pickup, manual override, and handoff completion. Success means customers understand their next step, operators can trust the screen in front of them, and every staff action is auditable and tied to a clear backend state.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The product should feel composed and trustworthy rather than decorative, with enough polish to match a premium airport mobility service.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboard decoration, generic card grids, oversized hero treatments, hidden operational context, and document-only queues that separate review decisions from the reservation and vehicle context. Avoid interfaces that ask staff to make irreversible decisions without showing the customer, vehicle, timing, and lifecycle state together.

## Design Principles

1. Put operational context before action.
2. Show backend truth, never pretend state.
3. Minimize sensitive data while preserving staff confidence.
4. Make the primary task obvious at airport pace.
5. Keep review, handoff, and override actions auditable and explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for authenticated product surfaces. Interfaces should remain keyboard-accessible, readable in bright environments, clear without relying on color alone, and respectful of reduced-motion settings.
